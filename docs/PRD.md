# Mali putnici / Little Travellers: PRD & handoff

> Status: **paused mid-build (2026-09-26)**. This file is written for the next AI agent or developer.
> Original concept (reference only, don't edit it): `../Porodicna Putovanja Sajt.html`

## 1. Product vision
A public, bilingual (Serbian Latin + English) inspiration site for **families with babies, toddlers and preschoolers (0–6 y)**.
It helps them choose a **safe, calm, genuinely kid-friendly trip in Europe**, with extra focus on the **Balkans, Greece and Romania**,
and tells them **roughly what it will cost their family**.
The main audience is parents in Serbia and the ex-Yu region (mostly flying or driving from Belgrade), plus international families.

The promise: *"Every place is checked, honest about its downsides, and priced for YOUR family."*

## 2. Target users & jobs
- A parent on a phone in the evening, planning a first flight with a small child: "Where can we go that works with a 1- and a 4-year-old, and what will it cost?"
- A couple shortlisting 2–3 options together: they save favorites, compare them side by side, and share a link.

## 4. Non-negotiables
- **Safe places only.** Honest cons (crowds, heat, stairs/cobbles, animal-welfare issues such as orca shows, seasonal closures).
- **No invented facts.** Prices, age limits and seasons come from sources; unverified prices are flagged `priceConfidence: "estimated"`. **Never claim specific direct flight routes.**
- **Licensed images only** (Wikimedia Commons, CC0/CC-BY/CC-BY-SA/PD), with attribution. No hotlinking.
- **Natural Serbian** (Latin, ekavian, č ć š ž đ): correct cases ("iz Beograda", "u maju") and collective numerals ("dvoje dece").
- Works by **double-clicking index.html** (file://), so no ES modules or `fetch()` of local JSON. Data is loaded as a `<script>` (`window.PP_DATA`).

## 6. Cost model (calc.js + pricing.json)
- Flight one-way per seat ≈ `50 + 0.03·km` (great-circle, origin airport → destination airport) × month season factor; range = ×0.55 (low) … ×1.85 (high); high end ×1.4 more if either airport is not a large one (connection likely).
- Kids 2+ pay a full seat (low-cost norm); babies <2 pay €28 per leg. One checked bag (€35/leg) per 2 travellers. Airport transfer: taxi-ish €15 + €1.3/km each way, capped at a rental-car cost.
- Drive: road km ≈ straight line × 1.28; fuel €0.14/km + average tolls; ferry extras for Corfu/Thassos/UK; fly-only islands (Crete, Rhodes, Naxos, Kos, Mallorca, Tenerife, Malta, Cyprus). Driving is suggested when road km ≤ 520.
- Stay: destination `hotelNight[tier]` (2+2 family, spring baseline) × seasonal factor (coast vs city curve) × family-size factor.
- Tickets: each main attraction once; kids under `freeUnder` go free. Food: `foodPerAdultDay` × (adults + 0.5·kids 2+ + 0.2·babies) × days. Local: `localPerDay` × days.
- Always shown as an **estimate range**, never as a quote.

## 7. Design direction
- **Signature element:** the fill-in hero sentence in big type, with inline "sticker" pills (sun-yellow). Each pill has a transparent native `<select>` over it, so the display text can use the correct grammatical case.
- **Palette:** cloud `#F4F7FB`, ink `#1C2340`, Adriatic teal `#0B8A8A`, sun `#FFC53D`, poppy `#EF4F5A` (hearts/cons), meadow `#3FA66A` (pros). Dark mode: night `#0F1428`, surface `#171D35`, teal `#3CC3BD`. CSS vars used in index.html: `--sun --ink --ink-3 --ink-fixed --hill-1 --hill-2 --sea`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display, 700–800) + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (body). Both cover Serbian diacritics.
- Left-aligned layout. Avoid templated tells: no ALL-CAPS eyebrows, no "01/02" numbering, no gradient washes, and not the same radius/shadow on everything (sheet 28 px > card 20 px > pills fully round).
- Cards: 4:3 cover photo with blur-up LQIP, heart + compare buttons, place + country, name, tagline, travel chip (plane or car + time from the chosen origin), "about €X for your family", stroller/age hints, and a warning badge if the place doesn't suit the chosen kids' ages.
